# Pre-Task Checklist (paste before risky sessions)

Before doing anything else, confirm the following back to me in 5 lines or less:

1. **Working directory** — what is it, and does it match the task?
2. **Files in scope** — list the exact file(s) you will read/edit. Nothing else.
3. **Task restated** — one sentence, in your own words, of what "done" looks like.
4. **Tools you expect to need** — name them. If "web search" is on this list, justify why local files can't answer it.
5. **Out of scope** — name one thing you will explicitly NOT touch (configs, infra, unrelated modules).

Do not read any file outside what you listed in #2 without telling me first.
Do not run a web search unless you stated it in #4 and I haven't objected.
If anything is ambiguous after reading the named files — stop and ask, don't guess.

Wait for my go-ahead after this checklist before making any edits.
